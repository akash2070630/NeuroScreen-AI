# NeuroScreen AI — Clinical & Research Limitations

## 1. Non-Diagnostic Scope
NeuroScreen AI is an academic and investigative research prototype. It is NOT an FDA, CE, or regulatory-cleared medical device. Under no circumstances should results be interpreted as confirming or excluding any neurological disease (including Parkinson's disease, acute ischemic stroke, Bell's palsy, or ALS).

## 2. Sensor & Environmental Variance
- **Acoustic Background Noise:** High ambient noise impairs autocorrelation-based pitch tracking and elevates jitter measurements artificially.
- **Lighting Dynamics:** Low illumination or directional lighting can cause landmark jitter and false asymmetry detection.
- **Camera Frame Rate Jitter:** Software recording on low-spec smartphones may experience dropped frames, altering kinematic derivative estimations.

## 3. Emergency Safety Limitations
The application cannot detect acute medical emergencies (such as acute ischemic or hemorrhagic stroke). Users experiencing sudden symptoms must never wait for app processing and must seek emergency medical services immediately.
